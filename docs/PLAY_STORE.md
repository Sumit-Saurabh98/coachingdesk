# PLAY_STORE.md

Everything for the Google Play listing of **Coaching Desk** (package `in.coachingdesk.app`, KB #55). The graphics are in `apps/mobile/store/`; re-render them with `node scripts/render-store.mjs` (in `apps/mobile`) after changing the website's app screens (`apps/web/illustrations`) or `store/source/store.html`.

Lengths below are checked against Play's limits. Play shows the Hindi listing to phones set to Hindi; everyone else sees English.

## 4. Data safety form (draft: check every answer before submitting)

What the app collects is stored on our servers to run the service. Nothing is sold, used for ads, or shared with third parties for their own purposes. WhatsApp (Meta) delivers messages **as our service provider**, which Play does not count as "sharing".

| Data type (Play's names) | Collected | Why | Optional? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info → Name | Yes: the user's name; students' and parents' names they enter | App functionality, account management | User's own name: optional. Students' names: needed |
| Personal info → Phone number | Yes: the user's login number; parents' and students' numbers they enter | Account management (login), app functionality (WhatsApp messages) | Login number: required |
| Financial info → Other financial info | Yes: fee amounts, invoices and payments recorded by the institute (no card or bank details) | App functionality | Required for fees |
| App activity → Other user-generated content | Yes: attendance, test marks, notices | App functionality | Required for those features |
| App info and performance | No crash reporting or analytics yet (update this when Sentry is added, Step 40) | | |
| Location, contacts, photos, files, calendar, messages, health, web browsing, device IDs | **Not collected** | | |

- **Encrypted in transit:** Yes (HTTPS only).
- **Users can ask for their data to be deleted:** Yes: in the app (Me → Delete my account; owners: More → Your data → Delete institute) and at https://coachingdesk.in/delete-account.
- **Data about others:** institutes enter students' and parents' details. Under India's DPDP Act the institute is responsible for that data and we process it for them (KB open question 3; the privacy policy says the same). Parents get WhatsApp messages only after the institute records their consent.
