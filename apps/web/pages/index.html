What you can do

Students and batches

Add students one by one or paste a whole list. Batches with timings, roll numbers, parent and student phone numbers.

Attendance in seconds

Everyone starts present, tap the absentees. Works without internet and syncs later. Absence alerts go to parents.

Fees, reminders, receipts

Monthly, quarterly or one-time fees billed automatically. Polite WhatsApp reminders before and after the due date. A receipt after every payment.

Payments go straight to you

Parents pay you directly, in cash or to your own UPI ID. You record it in a tap. No payment gateway, no commission on your fees, no waiting for settlement.

Tests and results

Enter marks in roll-number order, see the class average, and send each parent their child's result card.

Notices and your team

Holidays, timing changes and messages to the batches you choose. Add teachers and front-desk staff with their own access.
